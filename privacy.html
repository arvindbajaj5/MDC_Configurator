Privacy Policy — AI Modular Data Center Configurator

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Local-First Data Storage

All project configurations, equipment rate cards, pricing margins, and uploaded company logos are stored locally in your browser (LocalStorage) on your device.

2. Optional Google Drive Synchronization

If you choose to sign in with Google OAuth 2.0, the application requests the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope solely to save and load your own JSON project backup files inside your Google Drive. No project data is transmitted to any third-party server.
